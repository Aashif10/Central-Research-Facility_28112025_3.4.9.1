Procedure/steps to avail the facility
  1. Make detailed engineering drawing of the job with proper tolerances
  2. Raw material will be arranged by the user
  3. Discuss the feasibility of the job with the operator/operational incharge with hard copy of drawing and raw material 
  4. If the job is feasible, the approximate number of hours required in the job will be informed by the operator/operational incharge
  5. Book the next available slot on CRF website 
  6. Be present at the lab location on your slot timing 
  7. Get your work done.

Lathe Machine

A machine tool that rotates a workpiece about an axis of rotation to perform various operations such as threading, parting, knurling, drilling, facing and turning.

Capabilities

  • High speed with precision. 
  • Dry sump lubrication for minimum thermal deformation.
  • Operations can be performed: Turning, Drilling, boring, threading, grooving, facing, knurling, chamfering, parting.

Sample type:

  • In the form of bar of any cross section like square, hex, circular etc 
  • Material: Metals, polymers, wood
Lathe Machine Company : HMT ltd. MODEL: NH22 , India

Shaper Machine

It is a Batlition Grade-I geared drive. Operations like Machining of horizontal and vertical surfaces, cutting slots and keyways, machining splines.

Capabilities

  • Batlition Grade I geared drive
  • Operations can be performed Machining of horizontal and vertical surfaces, cutting slots and keyways, machining splines, gears manufacturing.

Sample type:

  • In the form of bar/billet 
  • Material Metals, polymers, wood
Shaper Machine Company : ​Batliboi & Co.

Vertical Milling Machine

It includes both vertical and horizontal milling machine. Operations like machining of flat surfaces, cutting slots, dovetails, keyways.

Capabilities

  • Power operated feeds and rapid traverses in all directions 
  • 5 KW motor for heavy stock removal 
  • Wide range of spindle speeds and feeds 
  • Operations can be performed Machining of horizontal and vertical surfaces, cutting slots and keyways, machining splines

Sample type:

  • In the form of bar/billet/rod 
  • Material : Metals, polymers, wood
Vertical Milling Machine : ​ HMT ltd India MODEL: ​FN3V

Horizontal Milling Machine 

It includes both vertical and horizontal milling machine. Operations like machining of flat surfaces, cutting slots, dovetails, keyways.

Capabilities

  • Wide range of spindle speeds and feeds 
  • Operations can be performed Machining of flat surfaces, dovetails, keyways, and gears

Sample type:

  • In the form of bar/billet/rod 
  • Material : Metals, polymers, wood
Horizontal Milling Machine : ​ HMT ltd India MODEL: ​FN3V

Drilling Machine

It is a pillar type drilling machine that can perform operations like drilling, reaming, boring, counter-boring, counter-sinking, spot facing.

Capabilities

  • Pillar type Upto 25 mm diameter hole 
  • Operations can be performed drilling, reaming, lapping, boring, counter boring, counter sinking, spot facing.

Sample type:

  • In the form of bar/billet/rod
  • Material : Metals, polymers, wood
Drilling Machine Make : ​ ​Bhavya machine tools India
