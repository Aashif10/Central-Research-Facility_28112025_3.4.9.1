Refrigerated Centrifugal Concentrator  (Cell -Biology Lab Sonipat Campus) 

The Refrigerated CentriVap Centrifugal Concentrator is designed to rapidly concentrate multiple small heat-sensitive samples, such as DNA, RNA and proteins using centrifugal force, vacuum and controlled temperature. This exclusive concentrator combines a refrigeration system that cools to -4oC with a heater that warms to 100oC to achieve the user’s desired set point temperatures.
