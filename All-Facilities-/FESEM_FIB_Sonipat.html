FESEM Helios 5 PFIB ( CRF Owned Facility Sonipat Campus)

  • The Helios 5 Plasma Focused Ion Beam (PFIB) Workstation is a fifth-generation, fully digital Extreme High Resolution (XHR) Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscope (FESEM) integrated with an Inductively Coupled Plasma (ICP) Focused Ion Beam (PFIB) column.
  • This advanced system enables high-precision imaging, analysis, and sample preparation for a wide range of materials research applications.
  • The instrument facilitates in-situ large-area sample preparation and high-resolution imaging and analysis over areas spanning hundreds of microns, ensuring the collection of relevant and representative results.
  • It offers high-performance site-specific material processing and high-volume material removal, making it suitable for applications such as top-down deprocessing, cross-sectioning, and rapid characterization of nanoscale features.
  • The system also supports 2D and 3D analysis, high-quality TEM sample preparation, and in-situ materials testing.

Model: Helios 5 PFIB CXe
