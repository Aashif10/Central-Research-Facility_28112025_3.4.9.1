FPLC (CRF Shared Facility ) DBEB Instrumtation Lab IIT Delhi

Fast protein liquid chromatography (FPLC) is a type of liquid chromatography that provides high resolution for protein characterization and separation. AKTA explorer 100 features high loading capacity, fast flow rates (up to 100 ml/min) for different common chromatography modes (e.g., gel filtration, ion exchange, reversed phase, and affinity chromatography). The system allows the users to monitor UV, pH and conductance along with high level of automation including samplers, gradient program control, and peak collection which enables reproducible results. The method is applicable to proteins as well as other kinds of biological samples including oligonucleotides and plasmids.

Column available with FPLC:
  1. SP-Sepharose Fastflow (Column Volume: 5mL)
  2. Q-Sepharose Fast flow (Column Volume: 5mL)
  3. DEAE-Sepharose-16/10 (Column Volume: 20mL)
Sample Conditions for FPLC
  1. For quantitative analysis, you need to provide standards for the respective analyte(s).
  2. All the standards will be consider as sample and charged accordingly.
  3. All the samples (standards + unknowns) will be processed using the method provided by you depending on the availability of the column*.
  4. We need minimum 2 and maximum 10 mL liquid sample filtered through 0.2 µm filter.
  5. All the samples and Buffers should be in aqueous phase only.
  6. We will provide you raw data only.
  7. We will process the samples only when we got Min 20 Samples for testing.
