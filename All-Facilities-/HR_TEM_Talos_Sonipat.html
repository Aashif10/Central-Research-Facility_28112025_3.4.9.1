HR-TEM Talos F200X G2(S) 200 KeV ( CRF Co-Owned Facility Sonipat Campus)

Model: Talos F200X G2 (S) TEM

Make: ThermoFisher Scientific

  • The Talos F200X G2 Scanning Transmission Electron Microscope ((S)TEM) provides precise and nanometer-level quantitative characterization It incorporates innovative features designed to enhance throughput, precision, and ease of operation.
  • Schottky X-FEG electron source operated at accelerating voltages of 60, 80, 120 or 200 kV
  • The Thermo Scientific Talos™ F200X G2 (S)TEM combines high-resolution TEM and (S)TEM imaging with advanced Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS) signal detection (Super-X G2), enabling advanced 3D chemical characterization and compositional mapping.
  • The system is equipped with a 4k × 4k Ceta 16M camera (capable of acquiring 40 fps (and 512 x 512 pixels at 320 fps)), offering a large field of view, fast image acquisition, and high sensitivity, supported by a 64-bit platform for efficient data handling.
  • Specimen Holder: High-visibility, low-background double-tilt holder, allowing improved sample positioning and imaging flexibility.
  • The Super-X EDS detector enables accurate chemical analysis at lower electron doses, making it suitable for a wider range of materials, including beam-sensitive samples.
