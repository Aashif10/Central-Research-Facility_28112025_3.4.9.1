LCMSMS (QQQ) (ULTIVO)

Company: Agilent

Country: Singapore

Brief Description of the Instrument and its potential use in research:

The Ultivo Model LCMSMS of make Agilent is a compact qualitative and quantitative analytical instrument that combines liquid chromatography physical separation with mass spectrometry’s high sensitivity and specificity for identifying and quantifying molecular mixtures. It ionises analytes and analyses based on mass to charge ratio.

Applications:

  • • Clinical research and toxicology
  • • Pharmaceutical drugs and impurity profiling
  • • Environmental and Safety
  • • Metabolomics

Sample requirements & preparation:

LC–MS samples should be high purity (analytical grade or better) and prepared in volatile, LC-compatible solvents such as acetonitrile, methanol, or water. Samples must be completely miscible, salt-free, and 0.2 µm filtered, with a typical concentration range of 0.1–1 mg/mL and submitted in clean glass vials. A detailed description of the sample, preparation, and any specific method requirements should be provided; otherwise, standard methods may be applied.

Forbidden samples:

Avoid inorganic buffers, high salt concentrations, non-volatile ion-pairing agents, viscous liquids, particulates (microcrystals), aggregated proteins, surfactants/detergents, plasticizers, strong acids/bases outside column pH limits, low-grade solvents, and immiscible solvents. These can cause column clogging, high backpressure, signal suppression, contamination, or damage to the LC–MS system.
