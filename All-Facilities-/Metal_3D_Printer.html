Metal 3D printing facility  (CRF Shared Facility) IITD

Equipped with a 400 W single mode Yb-fiber laser, this state-of-the-art metal 3d printer allows precise deposition of metal parts down to the accuracy of hundreds of microns in a controlled environment. The generic process based on which the printer works is laser powder bed fusion. The system allows the deposition of a wide variety of metal powders, such as different grades of stainless steel, titanium alloys, nickel, copper alloys, refractory, and custom materials. With future upgrades, it is even possible to monitor and control each layer while the deposition occurs. The technology can be used for a variety of applications, including but not limited to aerospace, defense, biomedical, advanced manufacturing, etc. 

Model : EOS M290, Company: EOS GmbH, Germany
Requirment for ​Metal 3D printing Job
  • Material: Stainless Steel 316L
  • The user shall prepare a 3D model of the job in .STL format using any CAD software as per their requirements.
  • The same needs to be emailed to the operator in charge to check the feasibility of printing the job.
  • For any further queries, please get in touch with the faculty coordinator through email.
Metal ​3D Printing Job Charges
Booking charge for Metal 3D Printing: Rs. 600/-. This is the standard booking cost of the facility to check the feasibility of the job. The user is advised to meet the operator-in-charge with the CAD file (in.stl format) of the part to be printed to know the feasibility.  After the feasibility is established, the user will be provided with the actual total cost, which depends on the material consumption, time required to complete the job, consumable gases, etc.  Once the amount is confirmed, users will be provided with further instructions on how to carry out the job. Non-IITD users may contact the faculty coordinator / operator-in-charge charge for information on the charges. External Users have to pay 18% GST Extra.
