ENERGY DISPERSIVE X-RAY MICROANALYSIS (EDX)

This is a non-destructive analytical technique used for elemental analysis and chemical characterization of materials. It can provide 129 eV intensity peak (energy) resolution, as well as optimized detection of low energy X- rays from light elements.

Some salient features of EDX analysis of known or unknown materials:

  • Qualitative and quantitative analysis for all elements from carbon upto uranium in the periodic table.
  • Detection limits typically 0.1 – 100Wt% for most elements.
  • Elemental maps, line scans and spectra can be obtained.

Sample Requirements for SEM & EDX

  • General Size: Any dimension (Height or Diameter): Less than 10 mm.
  • Conductivity (Electrical): The sample should be conducting or at least semiconducting. If the sample is not electrically conducting, it will require a gold coating for imaging.
  • EDX Analysis: If the sample is a powder, it should be prepared as a normal button-sized pellet.
Note: Samples should be 100% moisture-free. Oily samples will not be accepted for SEM / EDX analysis. There is no environment and low vacuum mode available in this instrument.
