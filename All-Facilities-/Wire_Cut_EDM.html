WIRE ELECTRO-DISCHARGE MACHINE (CRF Shared Facility IIT Delhi)

Wire Cut EDM provide a wide range of opportunities and possibilities in cutting intricate shape and size with precise cutting speed, giving a clear advantage over conventional cutting equipment’s. In this process, a thin Mo metallic wire of diameter 0.18 mm is fed on to the workpiece which is held in a tank and the deionized water is used as a coolant fluid. The wire is fed continuously through the spool between the upper and lower guide ways. The sample which is cut will have high heat and temperature which melts and remove the workpiece for required dimensions.

Instrument Make : Medha Industries, Kanpur, India

About Samples

The user shall prepare a 2D model of the job in AutoCAD software as per their requirements. In general, wire cut EDM can be used to cut materials with regular geometries, such as cylindrical or flat samples. Kindly discuss with the operator- in-charge about the maximum thickness of the material before booking the facility.  Materials: Conductive metallic samples only.
